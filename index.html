Privacy Policy

GoAthlete for Digital Solutions

Effective Date: OCTOBER 2026

GoAthlete is a digital sports platform that enables athletes to build a sporting identity, record and showcase their journey, share performance information and media, and connect with athletes, teammates, coaches, scouts, clubs, parents/guardians, friends, and fans.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, what may be visible to others, and the choices and rights available to users.

1. Who We Are

GoAthlete for Digital Solutions (“GoAthlete”, “we”, “us” or “our”) operates the GoAthlete application, website and related services (the “Services”). GoAthlete is responsible for the personal data it processes, subject to applicable data protection laws.

Privacy contact: info@goathlete.co

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use GoAthlete, we may collect:

  • Account information such as name, username, email, mobile number, date of birth/age, country, language, account type and login credentials.
  • Sporting profile information such as sport, position/discipline, age group, club/team, sporting history, statistics, results, achievements, rankings, coach endorsements and sporting goals.
  • Content such as profile photos, videos, highlights, posts, comments, reactions, performance entries and other content you choose to upload or share.
  • Verification information such as information used to verify identity, age, sporting status, coach/scout status, club affiliation or performance. Verification evidence may include identification or supporting documentation and is not displayed publicly.
  • Communications such as messages, contact requests and communications made through the Services.
  • Technical and usage information such as IP address, device type, operating system, app version, device identifiers, approximate location, logins, feature usage, security logs and crash information.

3. Public Sporting Profiles & Private Information

GoAthlete is designed to help athletes be seen (by themselves and others). Depending on age, settings and applicable law, a sporting profile may display a name/display name, profile photo, sport, position or discipline, age group, club/team, statistics, results, achievements, sporting history, approved photos/videos, endorsements and verification status.

The following are not public by default: personal email, mobile number, exact date of birth, home address, precise location, passwords, identification or verification documents, security information, private messages and reports/moderation records.

Users should only place information in public profile fields that they are comfortable making visible. Privacy and visibility settings may be changed where the feature and applicable law permit.

4. Children & Teen Accounts

GoAthlete applies enhanced protections to users under 18. Teen profiles are set to high privacy by default. We may use age information and reasonable age-assurance measures to apply age-appropriate protections.

Where applicable law requires parental or legal guardian consent, GoAthlete will request and record that consent before the relevant account, processing activity or feature is activated. The applicable consent age may differ by country or region.

For younger users, GoAthlete may require guardian approval before a profile becomes discoverable or before other higher-risk features are enabled. Minor accounts may also have restricted messaging, contact, location, search, advertising or profiling functionality.

A public or discoverable minor profile means a sporting profile may be visible; it does not make protected contact information, precise location, verification documents or private communications public.

5. Messaging, Coaches, Scouts & Contact

GoAthlete may allow users to message or request contact with other users. Private messages are not public. GoAthlete may process communications using automated systems and, where necessary, authorized personnel to deliver messages, prevent fraud or spam, protect users, investigate reports, moderate abuse, enforce our rules and comply with law.

GoAthlete does not automatically disclose an athlete’s private telephone number or email address to coaches, scouts, clubs or other users. Additional safeguards may apply when an adult seeks to contact a minor, including contact requests, verification requirements, guardian controls, blocking and reporting.

6. Photos, Videos & User Content

Users retain ownership of content they upload. By using GoAthlete, users permit us to host, store, process, display and transmit their content as necessary to provide the Services and according to their selected visibility settings.

Users must have the necessary rights or permissions to upload content involving other people.

Use of content for promotion. Content uploaded by users may be displayed and processed as necessary to operate the GoAthlete Services in accordance with our Terms of Use. Where GoAthlete wishes to use an identifiable user’s image, photograph, or video in GoAthlete advertising or promotional campaigns outside the ordinary operation of services, GoAthlete will obtain appropriate additional authorization. Where the user is a minor, authorization will be obtained from the parent or legal guardian where required by applicable law and GoAthlete’s safeguarding policies.

7. How We Use Information

We may use personal data to:

  • create, secure and manage accounts and sporting profiles;
  • provide discovery, communication, verification, statistics, history and performance features;
  • personalize relevant features and provide sporting insights or recommendations;
  • operate safety, moderation, reporting, fraud-prevention and security systems;
  • provide support, maintain and improve the Services, and perform analytics;
  • communicate important service, security or legal information; and
  • comply with law, protect users and enforce our Terms, Community Guidelines and other policies.

8. AI, Analytics & Automated Systems

GoAthlete may use automated systems or artificial intelligence for performance summaries, statistical insights, recommendations, search and discovery, moderation, safety, fraud detection, translation and similar product functions.

Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain appropriate consent for materially different automated processing. Private messages are not treated as public content. If GoAthlete introduces materially different uses of personal data, including general-purpose AI training, we will update our notices and obtain consent where required.

9. Sharing of Information

Information may be shared with other users according to profile and content visibility settings; with clubs or organizations a user chooses to connect with; and with service providers supporting hosting, security, authentication, analytics, notifications, payments, verification, customer support or moderation.

We may also disclose information where required by law, to protect users or GoAthlete, investigate unlawful activity, prevent fraud or abuse, resolve disputes, or as part of a legitimate corporate transaction subject to applicable safeguards.

GoAthlete does not sell private athlete contact information or verification documents.

10. Marketing, Advertising & Location

Service and security communications may be necessary to operate an account. Promotional marketing will be subject to applicable law and available opt-out choices. Additional restrictions may apply to advertising or profiling involving minors.

Precise location is not public by default. Where a feature requires precise location, GoAthlete will request the appropriate device permission and any additional consent required by law.

11. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy and to meet legal, security, safeguarding, dispute-resolution and fraud-prevention requirements.

Users may request account closure and deletion. Following a valid request, GoAthlete will delete or anonymize personal data as required by applicable law, subject to limited retention where necessary for legal obligations, safety, investigations, security, disputes or legal claims. Some information may remain temporarily in backups, and content already shared with or copied by others may not be capable of recall.

12. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal data; withdraw consent where processing relies on consent; request a copy or portability of certain data; manage privacy settings; and complain to an applicable data protection authority. Parents or legal guardians may exercise applicable rights for a child where permitted or required by law.

Requests may be submitted to info@goathlete.co. We may verify identity or authority before acting on a request.

13. International Data Transfers

GoAthlete and its service providers may process information in countries other than the user’s country of residence. Where personal data is transferred internationally, GoAthlete will use appropriate legal mechanisms and safeguards required by applicable law.

14. Security & Safety

GoAthlete uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal data, including appropriate access controls, authentication, security monitoring and secure infrastructure. No digital service can guarantee absolute security.

Users may be able to block or report accounts, content, messages, impersonation, harassment or other inappropriate behavior. GoAthlete may review relevant information and take action to protect users and enforce its rules.
